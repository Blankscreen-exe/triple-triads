Triple Triad - UI themes

Mockups of the same screens (game board, lobby, trunk, create room, rematch) in different styles. Card art, backgrounds and frames are placeholders; in the real game they come from each player's asset pack.

Balamb Classic

Inspired by FF8's menus: blue-grey gradient windows with light borders, a wooden board and cream-gold accents.
Neon Arcade

Dark grid background, cyan for your side and magenta for the opponent, glowing text and edges.
Parchment Tabletop

Fantasy board-game look: parchment panels on a leather table, a green felt board in a wooden rim, gold-framed cards and wax-seal red.
Clean Light

Modern and minimal: white panels, soft shadows, indigo accents. Easiest to read and to build on.
